## Exact

CP-SAT, MIP branch-and-cut, Dijkstra on valid graphs — can claim OPTIMAL when status says so.

## Heuristic

ALNS, local search, Routing metaheuristics — often FEASIBLE / best-so-far; do not overclaim proven optimal.

## Product claim ladder

Gate and solution `status` / `meta.proven_optimal` beat marketing language in skills or papers.
