## Abstract / snippet

The Moving Target Vehicle Routing Problem (MT-VRP) seeks trajectories for several agents that intercept a set of moving targets, subject to speed, time window, and capacity constraints. We introduce an exact algorithm, Branch-and-Price with Relaxed Continuity (BPRC), for the MT-VRP. The main challenge in a branch-and-price approach for the MT-VRP is the pricing subproblem, which is complicated by moving targets and time-dependent travel costs between targets.
