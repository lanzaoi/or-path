## Abstract (from public metadata APIs)

We present a brief survey of the current state of the art in network reliability. We survey only exact methods and do not consider Monte Carlo methods. Most network reliability problems are, in the worst case, NP-hard and are, in a sense, more difficult than many standard combinatorial optimization problems.
