## Abstract (from public metadata APIs)

This paper proposes a new class of cutting planes for integer programming. A typical member of the class is generated as follows. Let X be the feasible set, and x̄ the optimal (noninteger) solution to the linear program associated with an integer program in n-space. Consider a unit hypercube containing x̄, whose vertices are integer, and the hypersphere circumscribing the cube. This hypersphere is intersected in n independent points by the n halflines originating at x̄ and containing the n edges
