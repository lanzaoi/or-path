## Abstract (from public metadata APIs)

AbstractGrid graphs are a simple class of planar graphs for which the vertices can be assigned integer coordinates so that neighbors agree in one coordinate and differ by one in the other coordinate. Grid graphs arise in applications from the layout design of integrated circuits to idealized models of city street networks.
