## Abstract (from public metadata APIs)

The Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP) is one of the classic and hard problems in combinatorial optimization. We develop a new heuristic that uses a connection between Minimum Cost Flow Problems and the TSP to improve on a given suboptimal tour, such as a local optimum found using a classic heuristic.
