## Abstract (from public metadata APIs)

Given a connected graph $G$ and a terminal set $R \subseteq V(G)$, the Steiner tree problem (ST) asks for a tree that spans all of $R$ with at most $r$ vertices from $V(G)\backslash R$, for some integer $r\geq 0$. It is known from (Garey et al.,1977 ) that ST is NP-complete. A Steiner tree in which all terminal vertices are constrained to be leaves is called a terminal Steiner tree.
